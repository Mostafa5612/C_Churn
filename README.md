Bank Customer Churn Analysis
Project Overview
This project looks into why customers leave the bank and what patterns stand out among those who churn. By analyzing customer behavior and visualizing key trends, the goal is to turn raw data into practical insights that can help the bank improve retention.

The Business Problem
Losing customers directly hurts the bank’s revenue and long-term growth. Right now, it is unclear which specific customer segments are leaving and what factors drive their decisions. This project aims to bring clarity to customer churn so the bank can make smarter, data-backed decisions to keep clients engaged.

Project Goals

Check and clean the customer dataset to ensure high data quality.

Spot key trends and major factors linked to churn.

Compare churn rates across different groups (by age, location, product count, etc.).

Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for clear visual reporting.

Train and evaluate a machine learning model to predict which customers might leave.

Deliver clear business recommendations based on the final findings.
